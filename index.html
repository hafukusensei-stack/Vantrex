Stochastic Optimization
We formulate industrial control problems as stochastic programs and solve them in real time using custom solvers — not off-the-shelf optimizers. Our methods handle uncertainty explicitly, producing solutions robust to measurement noise and disturbances.
Anomaly Detection Engines
Unsupervised and semi-supervised anomaly detection across multi-variate sensor streams. Our models are trained incrementally on-device, adapting to machine drift and environmental change without requiring cloud connectivity or labeled datasets.
Predictive Analytics Infrastructure
Time-series forecasting pipelines designed for industrial sensor data — handling irregular sampling, missing readings, and sensor degradation. Deployed at the edge with model compression for sub-millisecond inference on constrained hardware.
Decision Systems Under Uncertainty
Bayesian decision frameworks for applications where the cost of error is asymmetric — inspection, navigation, quality control. Our systems quantify what they don't know and escalate accordingly, eliminating silent failure modes.

WHERE THE
FRONTIER
BEGINS

Vantrex maintains active research programs in areas where no commercial solution exists — building the science before building the product.

01
Neuromorphic Control Architectures
Research into spike-based neural network controllers for robotic actuator systems — targeting ultra-low power inference at 10× the efficiency of conventional deep learning controllers. Applications include extended-duration field robots operating on harvested energy.
ActiveRoboticsEdge AI
02
Self-healing Material Systems
Structural composite research combining polymer chemistry and embedded sensor networks to create materials that detect, localize, and partially repair mechanical damage autonomously — reducing maintenance intervals and extending service life in harsh environments.

High-Speed Tactile Sensing
Developing a next-generation tactile sensor array capable of 10kHz sampling across 1,024 contact points — enabling robotic hands to detect slip, texture, and material properties at speeds approaching human fingertip sensitivity. Currently at TRL 4.
